From a drone photo to land records, automatically.

Detect the bunds that divide farmland, group them into individual fields, and turn each one into a measured, GPS-anchored parcel — replacing the surveyor who would otherwise walk and measure the land by hand.

EPSG:3035 georeferenced 1 m / pixel area in ha + acres U-Net · ResNet-34 GeoJSON · GeoPackage · CSV
The approach

Let the AI see, let geometry measure.

We split the problem in two — and that division is the whole idea. The network does the one thing only a network can, and exact math does the rest, so nothing about the final numbers is a guess.

Neural network

Recognises the bunds

A segmentation model reads the raw imagery — shadows, crops, hedgerows and all — and marks where the ridge boundaries run, the way a person would spot them by eye.

Deterministic geometry

Closes, locates & measures

Classical geospatial math turns those lines into closed parcels, pins them to real coordinates, and computes exact area. Explainable, not a black box.

The pipeline

Eight stages, image to export.

Each stage feeds the next. The model is trained offline on real parcel data; everything downstream is deterministic.

Input
1
Drone / aerial imagery
GeoTIFF · RGB · 1 m GSD · EPSG:3035

A georeferenced photo of the fields — every pixel maps to a real-world coordinate, which is what makes real GPS and real acres possible.

2
Preprocess
rasterio · tile 512² · normalize

Large orthomosaics are cut into tiles the model can handle, and pixel values are normalised.

Detect
3
Bund detection — the AI
PyTorch · U-Net (ResNet-34 encoder)

The network predicts a per-pixel boundary map: where the bunds are. This is the model training on Kaggle right now.

Geo-process
4
Parcel extraction
distance transform + watershed

Bunds are thin and often broken, so a watershed floods each field across the gaps — turning lines into whole, closed parcels. The hard part, done right.

5
Vectorize
shapely · rasterio.features

Each field becomes a polygon instead of a blob of pixels.

6
Georeference + area
pyproj · EPSG:3035 (equal-area)

Polygons get real latitude/longitude; because the projection is equal-area, area comes out exact in hectares and acres.

Output
7
Overlay + export
GeoJSON · GeoPackage · CSV · PNG

GIS-ready files that open straight in QGIS, plus a visual overlay so a human can verify the result at a glance.

8
Web demo
Streamlit

Upload an image, watch the boundaries appear, read each parcel's area, and download the data.

What we're using

The stack, and why each piece.

Model
PyTorch · segmentation-models-pytorch (U-Net / ResNet-34)
The published state of the art for field-boundary delineation; ImageNet-pretrained means high accuracy from little data.
Training data
AI4Boundaries — real EU parcels, 1 m
Gives labelled bunds and a ground-truth metric, so there's no manual labelling and we can report accuracy.
Post-processing
scikit-image (watershed) · SciPy
Turns imperfect, broken bund lines into closed fields — where most naive approaches fail.
Geospatial
rasterio · shapely · geopandas · pyproj
Pixels → GPS polygons → exact area, and export to every standard GIS format.
Compute
Kaggle T4 (train) → laptop (inference)
A big GPU to train the model, then a self-contained laptop demo with no internet dependency for judging.
Interface
Streamlit
A live upload-to-result loop the jury can watch in real time.
